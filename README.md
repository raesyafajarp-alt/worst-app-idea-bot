## The pitch

> FridgeFeel is an app that lets your fridge text you about its feelings, then charges $4.99 a month for couples therapy between you and your leftovers.

One sentence, under 240 characters: a real-sounding product, a founder who
means every word, and a function nobody on earth needs.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the pitch to
`/output/entry.txt` (falling back to `./output/entry.txt` when `/output`
is not writable), asserting it is a single sentence of 1–240 characters.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat output/entry.txt
```
